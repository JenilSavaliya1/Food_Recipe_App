About This App

As a gym enthusiast and someone who is passionate about living a healthy lifestyle, keeping my nutrition on track has always been a priority for me. But planning meals, balancing macros, and finding new healthy recipes can get overwhelming — especially when you're trying to stay consistent both in the gym and in the kitchen.

That’s exactly why I built this Recipe Recommender app. It helps me track my calories, protein, fats, and carbs effortlessly, while giving me recipe suggestions tailored to my diet and fitness goals. Whether I need a high-protein meal for muscle recovery or a balanced meal for cutting, this app makes nutrition simple, enjoyable, and sustainable.
